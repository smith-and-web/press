The bundled fonts have their separate notices:

- Fraunces: `licenses/fraunces/OFL.txt` plus `EMBEDDED-NOTICE.txt`.
- Newsreader: `licenses/newsreader/OFL.txt`.
- Inter: `licenses/inter/OFL.txt`.

Keep the complete font notices with copies of the fonts. The font notices do not license the Kindling brand or grant trademark permissions. Provenance and source URLs are recorded in `docs/FONT_SOURCES.json` and `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.
